# H. Founder Review

**Scope:** three art-direction variations within *Hybrid Monumental Surrealism*, the recommendation, and the decisions only the founder can make.

---

## 1. Three art-direction variations

### Variation A: **Dawn Courtyard** (recommended)
- **Mood:** calm, warm, monumental, hopeful. Blue hour moving to dawn as the visitor progresses.
- **Architecture:** tropical brutalism in warm laterite-aggregate concrete; a courtyard plan; a perforated Seam Screen wall; deep reveals; selective surreal moments.
- **Materials:** laterite concrete (dominant), basalt, patinated bronze, smoked glass, dark terrazzo, odum timber.
- **Light:** cool sky + warm 2900 K seams; strong patterned shadow from the screen wall.
- **Ghanaian grounding:** strongest. Courtyard plan, tropical-modernist language, laterite, strip-assembly logic, permanent scaffolding.
- **Strengths:**
  - Distinctive and personal.
  - Recognisable in a single frame.
  - Bright enough to read on phones.
  - The dawn arc carries the story without words.
- **Risks:**
  - Concrete can read "heavy" if lighting is poor. Mitigated by warm bounce in the bakes and the screen pattern.
- **Mobile cost:** low–medium (baked, opaque, few transparent surfaces).

### Variation B: **Night Foundry**
- **Mood:** dramatic, cinematic, luxurious. Permanent night; interiors lit only by molten-warm seams and machine glow.
- **Architecture:** charcoal basalt masses, deep voids, black steel, bronze everywhere.
- **Materials:** basalt (dominant), bronze, blackened steel, smoked glass.
- **Light:** almost no ambient; strong local warm light; high contrast.
- **Ghanaian grounding:** weaker. Mainly lost-wax bronze craft; the courtyard plan stays but reads less.
- **Strengths:** the most cinematic; machines look precious; strong "look beneath" glow.
- **Risks:**
  - Too dark on phones outdoors.
  - Reads like a luxury-brand or watch campaign.
  - Hides architecture.
  - Dark scenes band badly in compressed lightmaps.
- **Mobile cost:** medium (needs bloom to sell the look, which is costly on low tier).

### Variation C: **Suspended Lightwell**
- **Mood:** serene, airy, minimal. Bright diffuse daylight; pale stone; floating masses in white space.
- **Architecture:** pale limestone-like concrete, frosted glass, thin bronze lines; masses floating in a bright void.
- **Materials:** pale concrete (dominant), frosted glass, light bronze.
- **Light:** soft overcast daylight, minimal shadow.
- **Ghanaian grounding:** weak. It risks being culturally neutral.
- **Strengths:** very legible; feels premium and clean; cheap lighting.
- **Risks:**
  - Close to the generic "white gallery / tech keynote" look.
  - Surreal floating reads as product-launch staging.
  - Low memorability.
- **Mobile cost:** lowest.

### Recommendation: **A, Dawn Courtyard**, borrowing one element from B
- From B: in the Laboratory and inside machine reveals, darker basalt and stronger warm glow make the mechanism feel precious.
- A is the only direction where **culture, story and technique reinforce each other**:
  - the courtyard plan organises navigation and streaming;
  - the dawn arc tells "curiosity → ambition → opportunity" without words;
  - the warm, mid-key lighting stays legible on phones.
